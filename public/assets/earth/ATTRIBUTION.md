# Earth texture

`earth-viirs.webp`: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, Blue Marble 2015.
Visualization credits: Amy Moran and Jesse Allen. Source imagery: Suomi NPP / VIIRS.

- Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30763/
- Download: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a030000/a030700/a030763/global_vir_2015287_mosaic_sm.tif
- Format: 8192 × 4096 WebP, quality 82, 6,761,404 bytes, resized from NASA's 10800 × 5400 TIFF with Sharp CLI. Original TIFF is not distributed.
- Compression command: `npx --yes --package=sharp-cli sharp -i <original.tif> -o earth-viirs.webp -f webp -q 82 resize 8192`.
- The 1K JPEG was visibly blurred at the close orbital framing. After inspecting 4K, the user explicitly requested even sharper Earth/clouds. The delivered 8K composite prioritizes that detail over the original small-payload target, with an 8 MiB compressed budget. Lower-capability GPUs downsample to their supported texture limit before upload.
- Retrieved: 2026-09-13. Bundled locally; no runtime external requests.

Earth and clouds come from this composite texture. Atmospheric layers are generated by the shader; the sky has no stars. The Earth-from-orbit composition is artistic, not an orbital simulation. No Moon assets are used.

The layered-atmosphere approach was informed by the user's `RotatingGlobe.tsx` in https://github.com/medenijazbec/geotagger-frontend . LSG uses an independent dependency-free WebGL implementation, not a copy of its React component.
